Pixellari, by Zacchary Dempsey-Plante.

The author's terms (https://www.dafont.com/pixellari.font): "100% Free". His
note: "Providing this for absolutely free, since I myself found it difficult
to find a font I liked that was completely usable in commercial projects.
All I ask is that I get credit for the making of the original font."

Used in Prosio unmodified: the game reads this file as it is and draws its
letters on their pixel grid when it starts.
